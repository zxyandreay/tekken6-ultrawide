# v1.2.0 AutoHUD research timeline

This timeline preserves the major milestones from the original HUD/AutoHUD research history that led to v1.2.0.

It is intentionally selective: tiny formatting/build commits are omitted, while commits that changed the technical understanding, introduced a new experiment, disproved a hypothesis, or established a checkpoint are retained.

The old research branches were deleted after release cleanup, but these commits were still addressable by SHA and were used to reconstruct the detailed record in [`v1.2.0-autohud.md`](v1.2.0-autohud.md).

No debugger IPs, workstation paths, or device/network identifiers are retained here.
